Try Voxi right here.

The actual Voxi window, running on sample files right in this page. Press ⌃⌥Space anywhere on your Mac and it opens with the cursor ready. Tell it what you want, or dictate with an app like Wispr Flow or Hex.

More things you can tell it.

No rules to set up and no syntax to learn. Voxi ignores the “please” and the full stop a dictation app adds.

  • undo last

    Changed your mind? Take it back.

    Your latest change reverses after its own preview. A whole batch comes back in one step.

  • delete dmg files from Downloads every 3 days

    Put the usual on a schedule.

    Add “tonight at 9”, “in 30 minutes” or “every Monday”. Repeating tasks find matching files at each run, and every run can be undone from History.

  • list files in Downloads larger than 500 MBdelete 4

    Point at a row.

    Lists are numbered. Follow up with the number, or ask for “the last file”. Listing never changes anything.

  • tidy the screenies

    Teach it your own phrasing.

    If a sentence misses and you rephrase it, Voxi can remember the version that worked. It remembers which “project folder” you mean, too.

  • run my usuals

    Your routine in one phrase.

    Save a list of commands as a shortcut in Settings. One phrase, one preview, one undo.

What Voxi won’t do.

Erase a file.

“Delete” means the Trash. A name that’s already taken gets a new one, shown in the preview, so nothing is overwritten.

Act before you look.

Every file, destination and name clash is listed first. Press Esc and everything stays exactly where it was.

Wander outside your folders.

Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, and the folders you add in Settings. Nothing else.

Send your commands anywhere.

Commands are understood on your Mac. No account and no cloud. Reports about wrong results stay as readable files you can clear.
