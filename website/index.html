Voxi

Tidy your Mac.
Just say so.

Move, rename, convert and clean up files in one sentence. See the plan first. Nothing changes until you press Enter.

Download for Mac Install with Homebrew

Free and open source. macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

Say it. See it.
Then press Enter.

No rules to set up and no syntax to learn. Use your own words, typed or dictated.

1

Say what you want.

Press ⌃⌥Space anywhere and type a sentence. Voxi ignores the “please” and the period your dictation app adds.

2

See every change.

Each file, each destination, and each name that's already taken, listed before anything moves.

3

Enter, or walk away.

Press Enter to run it. Press Esc and everything stays exactly where it was.

A helping hand.
You stay in charge.

Look before you leap.

See exactly which files will move, change names, or go to the Trash. Press Esc to leave everything as it was.

0

Files erased.

“Delete” means the Trash. Nothing is overwritten: a name already taken gets a new one, shown in the preview.

A change of heart? Undo.

One sentence reverses your latest change, after its own preview. A whole batch comes back in one step.

Your Mac. Your business.

Commands are understood on your Mac. No account, and no cloud round trip.

Only where you say.

Voxi touches Desktop, Downloads, Documents, Pictures, and the folders you add in Settings. Nothing else.
