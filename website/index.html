Press ⌃⌥Space, type or dictate with Wispr Flow, check the preview, and press Enter.

Dictating
Enter to preview.Settings

  • Nothing changes until you press Enter

    Every command shows exactly which files will move, get renamed, or go to the Trash before anything happens.

  • Talk or type

    The box is ready the moment it opens, so Wispr Flow dictation lands right in it. No microphone access needed.

  • Undo anything

    “Undo last” reverses the newest change. Deleting moves files to the Trash, never straight out of existence.

Say it like this

Plain sentences. Voxi ignores the “please” and the period Wispr adds.

  • move screenshots from today to ScreenshotsFiles today's screenshots, creating the folder if needed.
  • convert all HEIC in Pictures to jpgMakes JPG copies next to the originals.
  • merge PDFs in Documents named scan into scan-all.pdfCombines every matching PDF, in name order.
  • delete the Claude dmg from DownloadsFinds Claude.dmg even without the dot, then moves it to the Trash.
  • run my usualsRuns a shortcut you saved in Settings, as one preview with one undo.
