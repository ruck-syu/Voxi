Free and open source, for macOS 12 or later.

Enter to preview.Settings

  • Nothing changes until you press Enter

    Every command lists which files will move, get renamed, or go to the Trash. Press Esc and nothing happened.

  • Nothing is erased or overwritten

    “Delete” moves files to the Trash. A name that's already taken becomes report 2.pdf, and the preview tells you.

  • Undo is one sentence

    Say “undo last” and the newest change is reversed as one step, after its own preview.

Say it the way you'd say it

Plain sentences, typed or dictated. Voxi ignores the “please” and the period your dictation app adds.

  • move screenshots from today to ScreenshotsFiles today's screenshots, wherever they are, creating the folder if needed.
  • convert all HEIC in Pictures to jpgMakes JPG copies next to the originals.
  • merge PDFs in Documents named scan into scan-all.pdfCombines every matching PDF, in name order.
  • list files in Downloads larger than 500 MBShows numbered matches. Then “delete 4” or “move 5 to Desktop” acts on that row.
  • run my usualsRuns a shortcut you saved in Settings, as one preview with one undo.

Later, or every week

End a command with a time or a repeat. Voxi shows the preview now and runs it when it's due.

Repeating tasks match whatever is there at each run, and every run can be undone from History. Voxi needs to be running; a missed run happens once when it next opens.

  • move PDFs from Downloads to Documents at 9 pmOnce, tonight, using exactly the files in the preview.
  • clean up Downloads older than 90 days in 30 minutesOnce, after a delay in minutes or hours.
  • delete dmg files from Downloads every 3 daysClears installers on a schedule.
  • move screenshots from Desktop to Screenshots every Monday at 8 amWeekly tidy-up. Without a time, weekdays run at 9 am.

It learns how you talk, on your Mac

Voxi's parser runs locally and never sends your commands anywhere.

  • Teach it a phrase

    If Voxi misses a sentence and you rephrase it, it offers to run the working command the next time you say the original.

  • Name your folders

    When a name matches several folders, pick one and Voxi can remember it, so “the project folder” always means the same place.

  • Report a wrong result

    Reports are saved as readable files on your Mac. Open or clear them from Settings; nothing is sent.

Find just the files you mean

Combine a name or file type with a date or size. Voxi searches the folder you name and its subfolders.

  • Name or type

    PDFs, screenshots, “mov files”, or anything named invoice. Spaces, dashes, and underscores count as the same.

  • Date

    Today, this week, in August, before July 2026, or older than 90 days.

  • Size

    Larger than 100 MB, smaller than 2 gigs, or between 10 and 50 MB.
